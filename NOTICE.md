# Notices and rights boundary

Sabiqah contains materials with different rights. A repository checkout,
public URL, or deployment does not place every included or referenced item
under one license.

## Sabiqah software

Sabiqah-authored source code, scripts, tests, infrastructure-as-code,
configuration, and code-adjacent implementation documentation are licensed
under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE.md), unless a file
states different terms.

## Sabiqah-authored published content

Sabiqah-authored translations and other Sabiqah-authored scholarly content
intentionally published by the project are licensed under
[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](CONTENT-LICENSE.md), unless a more specific notice applies.
Authorship, publication eligibility, and any required attribution must be
recorded per work; use the [rights-matrix template](docs/templates/rights-matrix.md).

## Third-party materials

Third-party materials are not relicensed by either Sabiqah license. They retain
their existing copyright status, licenses, attribution requirements, and use
restrictions. Consult the applicable source-authority record, release manifest,
rights matrix, and attribution notice before reuse.

In particular, the existing OpenITI source attribution and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
terms recorded in [`docs/attribution/al-isabah.md`](docs/attribution/al-isabah.md)
remain in force. The repository-level licensing change does not broaden,
narrow, replace, or remove those terms.

## Private evidence

Private research evidence is neither published nor offered under the
repository's public licenses. This includes restricted witnesses, non-public
comparison material, unpublished workflow evidence, rights-holder
correspondence, and access-controlled research artifacts. Public records may
describe non-sensitive provenance and rights conclusions, but must not expose
private evidence, private implementation details, access information, or
restricted expression.

No permission to access, copy, distribute, adapt, or publish private evidence
should be inferred from its existence, use in research, or mention in public
provenance.
